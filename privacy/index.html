Information We Collect & How We Use It

In order to give you the best possible experience using our Games, we collect information from your interactions with our network. Some of this information, you actively tell us (such as your email address, which we use to track your account or communicate with you). Other information, we collect based on actions you take while using our Games, such as which rooms, spaces, or areas you visit and your interactions with our features (like favorites, friends, blocking, etc.) This information includes a record of those interactions, your Internet Protocol address, information about your device (such as your device identifier), and your platform user identifier (such as your Meta Quest user identifier). The specific information collected may vary from Game to Game.

We use this information to:

  • keep track of which user is tied to which account
  • prevent spam and other forms of abuse
  • generate statistics on how our Games are being used

Third-Party Services

Our Games rely on third-party service providers to deliver core functionality such as multiplayer networking, backend/account services, and VR platform integration. The providers used may vary from Game to Game. When you use our Games, certain information is shared with these providers as described below. We encourage you to review each provider's privacy policy, as we do not control their data practices and are not responsible for how they handle your information once shared.

Photon (Exit Games GmbH)

We use Photon's networking services to power real-time multiplayer connectivity in our Games, including room/session matchmaking, voice proximity, and object synchronization between players.

Data shared with Photon may include your device identifier, IP address, session/connection data, and in-game networking events (such as position and interaction data needed to sync gameplay between users). Photon may also collect technical/usage data on their own behalf as described in their policy.

Photon's Privacy Policy: https://dashboard.photonengine.com/account/privacyandcookiepolicy

PlayFab (Microsoft)

We use PlayFab for backend services such as player accounts, authentication, cloud data storage, and economy/inventory management (including virtual currencies such as Prism Crystals and Shards in Prism VR).

Data shared with PlayFab may include your account identifiers, login credentials/tokens, purchase and virtual currency transaction history, player statistics, and other data necessary to maintain your account and in-game progress. Because PlayFab is a Microsoft product, this data is processed under Microsoft's privacy practices.

PlayFab/Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement

Meta

Because our Games are built for Meta Quest, we integrate with Meta's platform services for functions such as user authentication (Meta account login), entitlement checks, and access to your Meta Quest user identifier.

Data shared with or received from Meta may include your Meta account/user ID, device information, and entitlement/ownership data used to verify your access to our Games. Meta's own collection of data through your headset and account is governed entirely by their policy, not ours.

Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/privacy-policy/
